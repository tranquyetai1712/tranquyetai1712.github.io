Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

"Dang Video Ca Nhan" (the "App") is a personal tool operated by tranquyetai1712 (the "Owner"). It is used only by the Owner to publish the Owner's own videos to the Owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we access

When the Owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives, with the Owner's consent:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok;
  • the TikTok account's open_id and basic profile information (scope user.info.basic);
  • permission to publish videos (scope video.publish).

2. How we use data

The data is used only to upload videos and captions chosen by the Owner to the Owner's TikTok account and to check the upload status. The App does not read other users' data, does not run analytics, and does not show advertising.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored only on the Owner's computer in a file readable only by the Owner. The App has no server and no database; nothing is stored on this website.

4. Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok's official API. It is never sold, rented, or shared with any other third party.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they expire or the Owner deletes the local token file. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions.

6. Children

The App is not intended for use by anyone other than the Owner and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
